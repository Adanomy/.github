# Adanomy

Autonomous systems and fleet management.

We connect autonomy research with engineering practice:
developing software, integrating it into vehicles, and testing
systems on real routes.

## Research and products

- **[Adanomy Research](https://adanomy.com/en/research/)**  
  Research into autonomy and the engineering work that turns
  experimental ideas into working systems.

- **[Adanomy Logistics](https://adanomy.com/en/logistics/)**  
  Autonomous transport for industrial and warehouse sites,
  bringing together vehicles, autonomy software, and fleet coordination.

- **[FleetOS](https://adanomy.com/en/fleetos/)**  
  Dispatch and coordination for human-driven, autonomous, and mixed
  fleets. A standalone product, also part of Adanomy Logistics.
